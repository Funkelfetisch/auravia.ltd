About us

An independent studio with a product habit.

Auravia Ltd is a Cyprus-registered product company. We don’t do one thing — we find problems worth solving and build the product to solve them, end to end: the engineering, the design, the AI and the launch.

Our work spans living-room art, the job hunt, finding a home, personal health, astrology, event lighting and illuminated fashion. Different markets, one standard: thoughtful products that serve the people using them.
